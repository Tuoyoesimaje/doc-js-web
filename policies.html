Overview

Doc JS is laundry management software. It helps businesses organise customer orders, garment details, order progress and payment records.

Doc JS provides software only. We do not provide laundry, pressing, dry-cleaning, pickup or delivery services, and we never take custody of any customer’s garments.

Using Doc JS

Give us accurate account information, keep your sign-in details private and use the service lawfully. You are responsible for the records you enter and for everything done through your account.

When you give staff access, keep their sign-in details secure. Remove their access when they leave your business.

Free access and changes

Doc JS is free for now while we test it with real laundries. Features may change as we improve the software, and there may be short interruptions.

If we ever introduce pricing, we will tell you well in advance, so you can decide whether to continue.

Business records

Your business stays responsible for the information it enters into Doc JS, and you keep your rights to your records. You allow us to store and process that information as needed to run the service. A laundry must have a proper reason for entering its customers’ personal information.

Privacy

We use your account details and the information you enter only to provide and support the service. We do not sell your records or your customer lists. We share information only with the providers that run the infrastructure behind the app.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the service and the information it holds. No internet service can promise absolute security, so keep your sign-in details private and contact us if you suspect someone has accessed your account.

Availability and responsibilities

Software that is still being tested may have errors, change as it is developed or be unavailable for a while. Keep any extra records your business needs, and check important order and payment information carefully.

Contact and updates

If you have questions about these terms or need help with your account, reach out through the support features in the app. Terms and contact details will be updated once formal business registration is complete.
