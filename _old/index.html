Running a laundry business

Running a laundry is more than washing clothes.

Customers, garments, payments, staff and running costs all need attention. The difficult part is keeping the different parts of the business in order while the work continues.

01 / Keeping the shop in view

You cannot be at the counter every minute.

When someone else receives clothes or records an order, you still need to know what was entered and what happened. Doc JS is being built to let authorised staff handle their permitted tasks while the owner retains control of restricted areas.

Staff access • Order records • Owner oversight
02 / Customer balances

A regular customer still owes you, but brings more clothes.

It can be difficult to insist on payment when you know the customer well. Keep previous orders, recorded payments and outstanding balances together, so you can see what is owed before deciding how to handle the next order.

Customer history • Payments • Outstanding balances
03 / Business costs

The shop is busy, but what remains after the bills?

Power, fuel, detergent, packaging and other costs add up. Record your business expenses and review them alongside the orders and payments recorded in Doc JS, so you have a clearer picture of the business.

Expense records • Recorded payments • Business overview
Order Creation

Every order needs a clear record.

Record who brought the clothes, what was received, the agreed price and any special care instructions. Keep the garment details, tags and order information together so the team has something reliable to refer back to.

  • Keep garment details and care instructions with the order.
  • Generate or share the appropriate order receipt.
  • Return to the customer record when a question comes up.
Clear order details your team can check later.
