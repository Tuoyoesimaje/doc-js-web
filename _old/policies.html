verified_user EARLY ACCESS NOTICE

Doc JS is provided free during the early-access period through 31 December 2026. Important pricing and policy updates will be shared before this period ends.

01 SECTION 1

Overview

Doc JS is laundry-management software that helps businesses organise customer orders, garment details, order progress and payment records. Doc JS provides software, not physical laundry or dry-cleaning services.

CLARIFICATION OF SERVICE

Doc JS provides software tools for administrative record-keeping. Doc JS does not provide physical laundry, pressing, dry-cleaning, pickup, or delivery services, and does not accept custody of any customer garments.

02 SECTION 2

Using Doc JS

Provide accurate account information, keep your sign-in details private and use the service lawfully. You are responsible for the accuracy of the business records you enter and for activity carried out through your account.

Staff Access

When granting staff access to your Doc JS account or shared counters, ensure credentials remain secure. You are responsible for revoking access when staff leave your business.

03 SECTION 3

Early access and changes

Doc JS is offered free during early access through 31 December 2026. Features may change as the software is improved, and temporary interruptions may occur. We will communicate planned pricing changes before the free period ends.

Notice on Pricing

Users will receive advance notice of future pricing structure before any fees apply, allowing you to choose whether you wish to continue using the platform.

04 SECTION 4

Business records

Your business remains responsible for the information it enters into Doc JS. You retain your rights to your business records. You authorise Doc JS to store and process information as needed to provide and maintain the service. A laundry business is responsible for having an appropriate reason to enter its customers' personal information.

05 SECTION 5

Privacy

Doc JS uses account details and the operational information entered into the software solely to provide and support the service. We do not sell your records or customer directories to third parties. Any sharing with service providers is strictly limited to infrastructure necessary to maintain and deliver the application.

06 SECTION 6

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the service and the information it handles. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your account credentials private and contact us if you suspect unauthorised access.

07 SECTION 7

Availability and responsibilities

Early-access software may contain errors, change as it is developed or be temporarily unavailable. Keep any additional records your business needs, and check important order and payment information carefully.

08 SECTION 8

Contact and updates

If you have questions about these terms or need support with your account, please reach out through the application's support features. Terms and contact details will be updated once formal business registration is complete.
