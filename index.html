Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, for now. We are testing with real laundries and learning what owners need. If that ever changes, we will tell you well before it does. No card needed.

Do I need a laptop?

No. Doc JS runs in your phone’s browser and you can add it to your home screen. Printing depends on your device and printer.

What can I record in it?

Customers, orders, garment tags, work progress, payments, receipts and business expenses.

Can my customers get a receipt?

Yes. Every order can have a digital receipt. Tap to open WhatsApp, choose the customer and send it. No paper receipt booklets needed.
